# Project Database

Project Database is a first design of my social media website, designed to allow students at Cooks Hill Campus The Big Picture Educations school in Newcastle to find other students projects. This would allow for increased collaboration, ideas, and monitering of students projects. The new version of this design is My respository FIC.

### History
Luvium Started out as a thought based on turning the tree outside of advisory two into a digital website. From there I started designing it with wireframes making sure I knew what I wanted it to look like. From there I created my first prototype based off of Instagram and how it lays out its mobile app. However, having a website based on a mobile app can be confusing for some people as there is a standard to how websites are made. 
So, I designed a new version starting with the wireframes and building up a new design and layout based on the following websites: Google, Facebook, Instagram. (In that order)
My mentor at the University of Newcastle, Dr Jacqueline Bailey (Associate Lecturer,  School of Information and Physical Sciences) made a comment while I showed it off at my exhibition stating “That would be incredibly useful at the Uni”. Which I then pursued selling it to The University of Newcastle. 
I made a meeting with Professor Karen Blackmore (Acting Head of School, School of Information and Physical Sciences) and talked about Luvium and its future. From there my Uni mentor and I created a plan for the future.
I am going to create diagrams showing how Luvium is going to work then talk with two people in charge of Work Integrated Learning and Projects for Computer Science.
